## Proxy and VPN tools

- **[Algo](https://github.com/trailofbits/algo)** — Personal IPSEC VPN in the cloud. `Open Source`
- **[ClashBar](https://clashbar.vercel.app/)** — mihomo-powered menu bar proxy client. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[ClashX Guide](https://clashx.tech)** — Comprehensive tutorials, tools, and troubleshooting guides for ClashX proxy on macOS. Features a YAML validator, rule generator, and optimization tips. `Freeware`
- **[Cloudflare WARP](https://1.1.1.1/)** — Replaces the connection between your device and the internet with a modern, optimized protocol. `Freeware`
- **[DashVPN](https://getdashvpn.com/)** — Smart-routing proxy client with support for VLESS, Shadowsocks, and HTTPS subscriptions. `Freeware` `App Store`
- **[Hiddify](https://github.com/hiddify/hiddify-app)** — Multi-platform auto-proxy client supporting Sing-box, X-ray, TUIC, Hysteria, Reality, Trojan, SSH, and more. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[Jumper VPN](https://jumpervpn.com/)** — VPN client for Mac and other platforms with secure, fast VPN proxy.
- **[Lantern](https://getlantern.org)** — Free application that delivers fast, reliable, and secure access to the open internet. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[Mullvad VPN](https://mullvad.net)** — Privacy-focused VPN that requires no personal information, keeps no logs, and allows payments with Bitcoin Cash, Monero, and more. `Open Source`
- **[Outline](https://getoutline.org/)** — Makes it easy to create a VPN server, giving anyone access to the free and open internet. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[RerouteMe](https://nadenco.gumroad.com/l/rerouteme)** — An easy one-click macOS proxy configuration app. `Freeware`
- **[ShadowsocksX-NG](https://github.com/qiuyuzhou/ShadowsocksX-NG)** — Next generation of ShadowsocksX. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[ShadowsocksX](http://shadowsocks.org/)** — Secure socks5 proxy designed to protect your internet traffic. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[Shimo](https://www.shimovpn.com/)** — VPN client for Mac.
- **[SpechtLite](https://github.com/zhuhaow/SpechtLite)** — Rule-based proxy app for macOS. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[Surge](https://nssurge.com/)** — Web developer tool and proxy utility for iOS and macOS with advanced rule-based routing.
- **[Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/)** — Makes creating software-defined networks easy: securely connecting users, services, and devices.
- **[tinc](https://www.tinc-vpn.org)** — Secure mesh VPN software. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[Tunnelbear](https://www.tunnelbear.com)** — Simple VPN to browse the web privately and securely. Unblock websites around the world with apps for Mac, PC, iOS, Android, and Chrome.
- **[Tunnelblick](https://tunnelblick.net/downloads.html)** — Free, open-source graphical user interface for OpenVPN on macOS. `Freeware`
- **[VPN Bypass](https://github.com/GeiserX/VPN-Bypass)** — Menu bar app to route specific domains and services around your VPN. `Open Source` `Freeware`
- **[Windscribe](https://windscribe.com)** — Gives 10 GB of free bandwidth monthly with limited server location options on the free plan.
